# The terms of `crates/catalog`

These terms apply only to the files of `crates/catalog/**`.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| variant | A product in one dimension and one color. |
| label | A short text on the page of a product, for example "New". |
| price list | The prices of the variants for one area. |
