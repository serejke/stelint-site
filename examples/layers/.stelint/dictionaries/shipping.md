# The terms of `crates/shipping`

These terms apply only to the files of `crates/shipping/**`.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| carrier | The external service that moves a parcel to the customer. |
| parcel | The container that holds the products of one shipment. |
| shipment | The parcels of one order that the shop sends together. |
| label | The document on a parcel that gives the tracking number. |
| tracking number | The number that the carrier gives to a parcel. |
