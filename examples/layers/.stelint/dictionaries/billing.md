# The terms of `crates/billing`

These terms apply only to the files of `crates/billing/**`.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| invoice | The document that gives the total price of an order to the customer. |
| payment provider | The external service that charges the customer. |
| payment | The total price of one invoice that the payment provider charges. |
| refund | A payment that goes back to the customer. |
| to settle | To record that the payment of an invoice is completed. |
